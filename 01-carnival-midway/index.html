Who's Behind the Curtain

The Willy Wonka of San Francisco

For three decades, if you walked into an incredibly artful, immersive party, bar, or restaurant in the Bay Area, there's a good chance you were standing in a room Barron Scott Levkoff built. Puppeteer, mask-maker, clown, and immersive designer — he's spent 35 years turning ordinary spaces into mythic ones, and now he's bringing that same Goldfire to New Orleans.
