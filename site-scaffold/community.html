The Part the Clients Don't See

Built By the Community, For the Community

"The Mystic Midway is a community, a performance troupe, a roving carnival, and a strategy for connecting people in a whimsical, playful and creative way. It's made up of professional actors, dancers, clowns, mystics, social activists, musicians, costume designers, graphic artists and more." — Shareable.net

Before Goldfire ever walks into a corporate campus, it's been tested on a much harder crowd — fifteen years of performers, ensemble members, and regulars who show up because they want to, not because they're on a client roster. These are their words.
