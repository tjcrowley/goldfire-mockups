About Goldfire

Who's Behind the Curtain

The Willy Wonka of San Francisco

Barron Scott Levkoff — founder of Authentic Mask, MidwayVille, and Mystic Midway — is an innovator in prosocial experience design, event production, entertainment, and community building, with over 30 years in the educational, corporate, and immersive entertainment worlds. Starting in the late 1980s, he worked as a professional improv character actor and puppeteer, traveling the globe with his Carnival of Wonders Traveling Puppet Theater for audiences that now number over a thousand shows strong.

In recent years Scott has worked as a UX and social-interaction consultant, immersive event producer/director, interior designer, and entertainer — helping brands unlock their potential through purpose-driven, prosocial design, story, and play. He's the creator behind Goldfire Social, Spookeasy, Mystic Midway, MidwayVille, Carnival of Wonders Puppet Theater, and Goldhive, and helped build Future Champions, Lagunitas Beer Circus, and a long list of other event brands and experiences across three decades.

"If you've gone to an incredibly artful, immersive party in San Francisco in the last 3 decades there's a good chance you've witnessed the handiwork of Barron Scott Levkoff." — Broke-Ass Stuart
